View your rocket launch live!

Opens a dynamic graph that plots the data of your rocket as time passes, giving you a better visual of your rocket's trajectory.

Just plop this bad boy in your Openrocket plugins folder, restart Openrocket, then go Edit simulation > Simulation options > Add extension > Flight > Live Flight Viewer.

Edit the displayed graph and show flight events in the top bar. Click on a graph to create a marker; hover over a marker and press Backspace/Delete to remove a marker.
